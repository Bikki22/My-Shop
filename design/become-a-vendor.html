Frequently asked
Do I need a business registration to sell?
Individual artisans can start with just citizenship verification. A PAN/business registration unlocks the Gold tier and higher payout limits.
How fast do I get paid?
Starter vendors are paid weekly; Gold vendors twice weekly. Funds settle to your linked eSewa, Khalti, or bank account.
Can I sell outside Kathmandu Valley?
Yes — Bazaar partners with courier networks covering all 7 provinces, including Cash on Delivery in most districts.
